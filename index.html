Technology

Equipment matters only in what it lets us see.

01

Digital precision

Intraoral scanning and 3D imaging let us plan treatment against the actual anatomy rather than an impression and an estimate. Fewer appointments, fewer surprises mid-treatment.

02

Comfort first

Digital impressions replace most trays. Procedures are shorter, anaesthesia is targeted, and you are told what happens next before it happens.

03

Sterilisation

Instruments are cleaned, pouched, autoclaved and logged against each cycle. Single-use items are exactly that. The process is documented and available to see.

04

Treatment planning

Implant position, crown shape and bite are modelled before anything is prepared, so the plan can be reviewed with you and adjusted while it is still only a plan.

Dr. Sanjna Nayar

Prosthodontics & Implantology

Dr. Nayar’s work centres on replacing and restoring teeth — implants, crowns, bridges and full-arch rehabilitation — and on the planning that decides whether those results hold up over a decade.

She previously served as Professor and Head of the Department of Prosthodontics at Sree Balaji Dental College and Hospital, Chennai, and directs the Edentulism and Anodontia Research Centre. Teaching has left its mark on how she practises: treatment is explained in full, and the reasoning is offered alongside the recommendation.

Consultations are held in English, Hindi, Punjabi or Tamil.
